This reference covers the 13 metrics available for Mercado Ads in Master Metrics. Use it to understand which data you can pull and how each field is named.

**Available in:** Dashboards · Reports · AI assistant · MCP

## Metrics

Metrics are the numeric values you can measure and aggregate from your Mercado Ads data.

| | |
|---|---|
| Impressions | **Field name:** `mercadoads_prints`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
