This reference covers the 87 metrics available for Shopify in Master Metrics. Use it to understand which data you can pull and how each field is named.

**Available in:** Dashboards · Reports · AI assistant · MCP

## Metrics

Metrics are the numeric values you can measure and aggregate from your Shopify data.

### Customers

| | |
|---|---|
| Count (Customers) | Number of store customers.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_customers_count`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |

### Abandoned checkouts

| | |
|---|---|
| Count (Abandoned Checkouts) | Number of abandoned checkouts.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_abandonedCheckouts_count`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Amount (Abandoned Checkouts) | Total amount of the abandoned checkouts.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_abandonedCheckouts_total_amount`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |

### Orders

| | |
|---|---|
| Count (Orders) | Number of orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_count`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Amount (Orders) | Original total price of the orders, including shipping, taxes and discounts, before edits and refunds.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_total_amount`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Fees (Orders) | Additional fees charged on the orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_fees`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |

| Shipping Cost (Orders) | Shipping charged on the orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_original_price`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Duties (Orders) | Duties charged on the orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_duties`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Price (Orders) | Current total price of the orders, after edits and refunds.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_price`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Average Price (Orders) | Average order value: sales divided by the number of orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_average_price`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Subtotal Price (Orders) | Price of the orders after discounts, before shipping and taxes.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_subtotal_price`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |

| Total Sales (Orders) | Total sales: net sales plus shipping, taxes, duties and fees.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_total_sales`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Net Items Sold (Orders) | Net items sold: product units sold minus units returned. Use it for products sold.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_net_items_sold`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Cost of Goods Sold (Orders) | Cost of the products sold, for products with a recorded cost.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_cost_of_goods_sold`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gross Margin (Orders) | Gross margin: gross profit as a percentage of net sales with cost recorded.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gross_margin`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gross Profit (Orders) | Gross profit: net sales of products with a recorded cost minus their cost.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gross_profit`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Amount Spent Per Customer (Orders) | Average amount spent per customer.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_amount_spent_per_customer`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Bundles Ordered (Orders) | Number of product bundles ordered.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_bundles_ordered`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Customers (Orders) | Number of customers who placed orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_customers`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Discounts Returned (Orders) | Discounts reversed by returns.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_discounts_returned`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gift Card Discounts (Orders) | Discounts applied to gift cards.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gift_card_discounts`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gift Card Gross Sales (Orders) | Gross sales of gift cards.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gift_card_gross_sales`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gift Card Net Sales (Orders) | Net sales of gift cards.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gift_card_net_sales`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gift Card Taxes (Orders) | Taxes charged on gift cards.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gift_card_taxes`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Gross Returns (Orders) | Gross value of the products returned.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_gross_returns`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Line Item Discounts (Orders) | Discounts applied to specific products.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_line_item_discounts`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Net Returns (Orders) | Value of the products returned, net of their discounts.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_net_returns`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Net Sales With Cost Recorded (Orders) | Net sales of products that have a recorded cost.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_net_sales_with_cost_recorded`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Net Sales Without Cost Recorded (Orders) | Net sales of products without a recorded cost.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_net_sales_without_cost_recorded`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| New Customers (Orders) | Number of customers who ordered for the first time.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_new_customers`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Number of Orders Per Customer (Orders) | Average number of orders per customer.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_number_of_orders_per_customer`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Order Level Discounts (Orders) | Discounts applied to the whole order.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_order_level_discounts`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Order Sale Agreements (Orders) | Number of sale records of the orders (sales, edits and returns).<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_order_sale_agreements`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Orders First Time (Orders) | Orders placed by first-time customers.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_orders_first_time`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Orders Returning (Orders) | Orders placed by returning customers.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_orders_returning`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |

| Quantity Returned (Orders) | Product units returned.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_quantity_returned`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Rate of Staff Assisted Sales (Orders) | Share of sales made with the help of a staff member.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_rate_of_staff_assisted_sales`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Returned Quantity Rate (Orders) | Share of the units ordered that were returned.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_returned_quantity_rate`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Return Fees (Orders) | Fees charged on returns.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_return_fees`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Returning Customer Rate (Orders) | Share of customers who had already ordered before.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_returning_customer_rate`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Returning Customers (Orders) | Number of customers who had already ordered before.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_returning_customers`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Shipping Charge Discounts (Orders) | Discounts applied to shipping.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_shipping_charge_discounts`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Shipping Charge Taxes (Orders) | Taxes charged on shipping.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_shipping_charge_taxes`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Shipping Returned (Orders) | Shipping refunded.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_shipping_returned`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Taxes Excluding Gift Cards (Orders) | Taxes charged, excluding those of gift cards.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_taxes_excluding_gift_cards`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Tips (Orders) | Tips received on the orders.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_tips`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Returns (Orders) | Total returns, including shipping, taxes and fees returned.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_total_returns`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Sales First Time (Orders) | Total sales from first-time customers.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_total_sales_first_time`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Total Sales Returning (Orders) | Total sales from returning customers.<br/><br/>**Field name:** `shopify_orders_total_sales_returning`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
