This reference covers the 33 dimensions available for LinkedIn Ads in Master Metrics. Use it to understand how you can segment your data and how each field is named.

**Available in:** Dashboards · Reports · AI assistant · MCP

## Dimensions

Dimensions are the attributes you can use to segment and group your LinkedIn Ads data.
