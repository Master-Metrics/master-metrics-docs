This reference covers the 40 dimensions available for YouTube in Master Metrics. Use it to understand how you can segment your data and how each field is named.

**Available in:** Dashboards · Reports · AI assistant · MCP

## Dimensions

Dimensions are the attributes you can use to segment and group your YouTube data.

### Time

| | |
|---|---|
| Day | **Field name:** `day`<br/>**Type:** dimension |
| Week | **Field name:** `week`<br/>**Type:** dimension |
| Month | **Field name:** `month`<br/>**Type:** dimension |
| Year | **Field name:** `year`<br/>**Type:** dimension |
