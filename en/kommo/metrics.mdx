## Metrics

Metrics are the numeric values you can measure and aggregate from your Kommo data.

|---|---|

| Leads Value $ | **Field name:** `won_leads_acc_price`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Average Sales Time | **Field name:** `avg_lead_sales_time`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
| Average Close Time | **Field name:** `avg_lead_close_time`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
