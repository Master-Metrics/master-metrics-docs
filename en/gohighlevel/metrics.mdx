## Metrics

Metrics are the numeric values you can measure and aggregate from your GoHighLevel data.

### Contacts

| | |
|---|---|
| Count (Contacts) | **Field name:** `ghl_contact_count`<br/>**Type:** metric<br/>**MCP:** Available |
