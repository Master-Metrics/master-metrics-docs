## Accept every permission on the Facebook screen

The connection fails when any of the permissions requested in the Facebook login window is unchecked. If the source could not be added, connect it again and keep all permissions and all pages selected.

## Instagram accounts must be linked to a Facebook Page

Only Instagram business or creator accounts linked to a Facebook Page can be connected, and the Facebook user who connects needs access to that page.
